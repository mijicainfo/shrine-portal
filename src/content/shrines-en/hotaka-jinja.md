## History

Hotaka Jinja stands in Hotaka, Azumino, Nagano Prefecture. It is read "Hotaka Jinja." The official site names Hotakami no Mikoto as the enshrined deity and describes the shrine as standing in Hotaka, Azumino, at the center of Shinshu. Hotakami no Mikoto is described as an ancestral god of the Watatsumi sea-god lineage, and the Azumi people, his descendants, are said to have handled sea transport around northern Kyushu and traded with the continent. The shrine has three sanctuaries: Hotakami no Mikoto in the center, Watatsumi no Mikoto on the left and Ninigi no Mikoto on the right, with Amaterasu Omikami in a separate shrine and Azumi no Muraji Hirafu no Mikoto in the Wakamiya.

The official site gives no founding year. The shrine is listed as a Myojin Taisha in the Engishiki Jinmyocho (Encho 5, 927) and is said to have been revered by the imperial court as a major shrine of Shinano. The earliest surviving record of its reconstruction is the 1483 (Bunmei 15) "Sanno Hotakasha Gozogu Sadame Nikki," though the official site considers that such work was probably carried out much earlier. It is said to have rendered great service to the development of Shinano as a deity of industry, and is venerated as the chief guardian of the Japan Alps and a protector of traffic safety and industrial development. Visits for first shrine visits for babies, protection from misfortune and protection from all directions are also said to be common in recent years.

Besides the main shrine in Azumino, Hotaka Jinja has an "Okumiya" beside Myojin Pond in Kamikochi and a "Minemiya" on the summit of Mt. Okuhotaka. The official site describes the Okumiya as the chief guardian of the Japan Alps and a protector of travel by land and sea, and says that records such as the 1770 "Hotakadake Gozoei Hogacho" suggest it was already built before then.

## Highlights

The grounds hold two Azumino City-designated cultural properties, the "Zelkova west of Wakamiya" and the "Zelkova at Daimon" (both designated October 29, 2008), each said to be more than 500 years old (official site). The Wakamiya, north of the kagura hall, is lined with small massha shrines. The "Sengu-sai" rebuilding festival is designated a city intangible folk cultural property. As the photo shows, the grounds also hold the sacred boat "Hotaka-maru" (the name given in the Wikimedia Commons description).

The "Mifune Hall" in the grounds is a museum showing the boat-shaped floats pulled out at the festival and the traditional doll decorations designated as an intangible folk cultural property of Nagano Prefecture. According to the official site, it also displays ancient plows, old masks, magatama beads, straight swords and old documents, photos and information on Azumino's roadside Dosojin deities, and, on the second floor, votive Hyakunin Isshu pictures (a city-designated folk cultural property). It is open 9:00 AM–4:30 PM, with admission of ¥300 for adults, ¥200 for junior high and high school students and ¥100 for elementary school students (closed days are not stated).

Hand-stroking visits to the Dosojin are another feature, introduced on the official site for health and longevity and for marital harmony and good relationships. Dosojin talismans (¥1,000), charms (¥800) and ema (¥800) are among the items offered.

Myojin Pond, where the Okumiya stands, consists of the First Pond and the Second Pond and is introduced as a pond that does not freeze. The Minemiya stands on the summit of Mt. Okuhotaka (3,190 m); a new shrine was built on the true summit in July 2013, and a remote-worship shrine (Minemiya Yohaisha) was built in 2016 to mark the periodic reconstruction (official site).

## Visiting Tips

Worship is possible 24 hours a day. The shrine office takes reception from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, and prayer services run from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM (the FAQ page says 9:00 AM–4:00 PM, so the official pages differ). Prayer services need no reservation and are first come, first served, taking about 30 minutes. They are not held for about 30 minutes before a festival or wedding, and photography is not allowed during the service. Pets are allowed on a short leash, according to the shrine.

Goshuin are issued daily from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM at the shrine office. The regular goshuin and the Dosojin goshuin are ¥500 each and the embroidered main-hall goshuin is ¥1,000. Limited-edition goshuin are offered during the Ofune Matsuri period (September), from New Year's Day to mid-January, and on March 17 for the Hoshaku-sai, among others (official site). The Okumiya goshuin is ¥500 and the Minemiya goshuin is ¥1,000. Goshuin are not sent by mail.

It is a 3-minute walk from Hotaka Station on the JR Oito Line, and about 10 minutes by car from Azumino IC on the Nagano Expressway. The official site gives no parking capacity or fee. To reach the Okumiya, the official site gives about a 60–70 minute walk from the Kamikochi Bus Terminal (also described as about an hour from Kappabashi Bridge), with no buses or taxis. Private cars are restricted in Kamikochi. If you are coming from the Kamikochi side, leave plenty of time.

## Annual Festivals

- **New Year's Prayer Festival (January 1–8)**: Listed in the official annual events.
- **Setsubun-sai and Kamado-gami-sai (around February 3)**: Held at 4:00 PM.
- **Hoshaku-sai and Toshigoi-sai (March 17)**: Limited-edition goshuin are also offered.
- **Dosojin Festival (April 18)**: Held at 10:00 AM.
- **Kamikochi Opening Ceremony (April 27)**: Held at the foot of Kappabashi Bridge to pray for safety in the mountain season.
- **Great Purification and Chinowa Rite (June 30)**: Held at 5:00 PM.
- **Wind Chime Festival (early July to around August 20)**: Listed in the official annual events.
- **Hotaka Reisha Mitama Festival (August 15)**: Begins at 8:00 PM, with "Sankenichi Tezutsu" hand-held fireworks offered.
- **Ofune Matsuri (September 26 eve festival, 27 main festival, 28 day-after festival)**: The shrine's annual festival, described as a Nagano Prefecture intangible folk cultural property.
- **Japan Alps Mountain Accident Victims Memorial (October 7)**: Held at the memorial song monument.
- **Okumiya Annual Festival and Boat Ceremony (October 8)**: On Myojin Pond in Kamikochi, two boats with dragon and sea-bird prows circle the pond in the boat ceremony, followed by a memorial service at 11:00 AM.
- **Kamikochi Closing Ceremony (November 15)**: Held at the foot of Kappabashi Bridge.
- **Commemorative Festival of Rank Elevation and Niiname-sai (November 19)**: Held at the main shrine.
- **Great Purification and Joya-sai (December 31)**: Held at 3:00 PM.

There are also monthly events such as the Otsuitachi-mairi (1st of each month, from 8:15 AM, except in January) and the monthly festival (27th of each month). Dates and times change from year to year, so check the "Annual Events" page on the official site for the latest.
