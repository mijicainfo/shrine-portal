## History

Tsuna Shrine (Tsuna-jinja) stands in the Kamiooba district of Mashiko, Haga District, Tochigi Prefecture, and is known as a shrine tied to the Utsunomiya clan. According to Mashiko town's guide, Utsunomiya Asatsuna, the third head of the Utsunomiya family, was exiled to Tosa Province in 1194 (Kenkyu 5) on a charge of seizing public rice fields. He prayed to the Kamo Myojin deity of Tosa for a safe return and, so the story goes, was pardoned soon afterward and came home.

After returning, Asatsuna handed over the family headship and founded the family temple Jizoin, then invited the Kamo Myojin deity from Tosa to the neighboring land and built a shrine there (the building is dated to around 1195-96). This is said to be the beginning of Tsuna Shrine. Some sources list the enshrined deities as Ajisukitakahikone no Mikoto and Okuninushi no Mikoto.

## Highlights

**An Important Cultural Property main hall**: The present main hall is said to have been built in the Daiei era (1521-1528) of the Muromachi period. A three-bay nagare-zukuri building with a thatched roof, it is designated an Important Cultural Property of Japan. Though modest in scale, it is described as expressing the rustic yet vigorous character of Muromachi-period architecture, and its beautifully curved thatched roof is striking.

**The subsidiary Okura Shrine**: Within the grounds stands the subsidiary Okura Shrine, whose main hall (a one-bay nagare-zukuri thatched building said to date from 1527, Daiei 7) is also an Important Cultural Property. It is said to have been moved in 1909 (Meiji 42) to the Tsuna Shrine grounds from the main hall of an Atago Shrine.

**The sacred shii tree (town natural monument)**: A shii (Japanese chinquapin) tree said to have been planted by Utsunomiya Asatsuna himself, with an estimated age of about 800 years, is designated a natural monument of Mashiko Town.

**Location for the taiga drama Toyotomi Kyodai!**: According to the Tochigi Film Commission's location record, a scene for episode 3, "Kessen Zen'ya" (broadcast of the series began January 4, 2026) of NHK's taiga drama Toyotomi Kyodai!, in which Kokichiro and Nao talk in a temple precinct, was filmed at Tsuna Shrine. The page also notes that peony flowering branches were placed in the grounds for the shoot. It records this one scene.

## Visiting Tips

Visitors are directed to park at the Oba-no-Sato Koyukan (912 Kamiooba, Mashiko), close to the shrine. Past the first and second torii, climb the stone steps and the thatched-roof main hall comes into view. One visitor review puts the steps at about 100, so sturdy walking shoes are a good idea.

There is no admission fee for the grounds, but no official information on visiting hours could be confirmed. The goshuin is likewise unconfirmed (how it is issued, when, and the fee), so if you want one, it is safest to call the shrine at 0285-72-2593 beforehand.

By train, it is about 15 minutes by taxi from Mashiko Station (Moka Railway). By car, it takes about 25 minutes from Sakuragawa-Chikusei IC or about 30 minutes from Moka IC on the Kita-Kanto Expressway.

## Annual Festivals

- **Annual festival (second Sunday of November; November 8 in 2026)**: The preservation society performs Daidai Kagura. The kagura is said to have been learned by villagers in the Bunsei era (late Edo period) from the tradition of Oguri Naigu Daijingu in what is now Chikusei, Ibaraki, and is selected as one of Mashiko's "Mashiko Seken Isan" local heritage items.
