## How to Choose a Shrine for Enmusubi

The term "enmusubi" covers a lot of ground: some visitors are hoping for romantic success, while others are seeking good relationships in friendship or work. Because each shrine's blessing traces back to a different myth or legend, one approach is to choose your destination based on the specific kind of "en" you're hoping for.

Shrines that enshrine a married couple of deities, such as Izanagi Jingu and Taga Taisha, are said to hold especially strong blessings for marital harmony and lasting relationships, while shrines with a romantic legend of their own, such as Kifune Jinja, are considered particularly powerful for romantic success.

## Tips for Your Visit

Keep the following in mind when visiting a shrine to pray for enmusubi, for a more meaningful visit:

- **Make use of charms and votive plaques**: Many shrines offer amulets and ema plaques specifically for enmusubi. Handle them respectfully, with your wish in mind.
- **Follow proper worship etiquette**: The standard form — two bows, two claps, one bow — is common to nearly every shrine. Approach your prayer with a calm, composed mind.
- **Remember to give thanks**: Rather than only asking for something, it's also considered important to express gratitude for the connections and encounters already in your life.
