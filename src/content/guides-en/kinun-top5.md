## Tips for Visiting Money-Luck Shrines

Shrines known for financial-luck blessings come from all kinds of backgrounds: some enshrine Inari deities, others worship a wealth deity like Benzaiten, and some — like Kamochi Jinja on this list — owe their reputation simply to an auspicious place name or shrine name.

Many visitors time their trip to coincide with buying a new wallet or amulet. It's also popular to visit before purchasing a lottery ticket, or to receive a goshuin specifically blessed for a winning draw — styles of worship found at money-luck shrines all across Japan.

## Things to Keep in Mind When You Visit

- **Check the shrine office's hours**: Financial-luck charms and goshuin are only available while the shrine office is open. Be sure to check the hours in advance.
- **Plan a realistic itinerary**: Some shrines, like Kamochi Jinja, can be a bit inconvenient to reach by public transportation. Check nearby accommodation and transit options ahead of time.
- **Pair your visit with other sights**: Shrines like Enoshima Jinja and Imamiya Ebisu Jinja are easy to combine with a visit to the surrounding sightseeing area. Consider working one into your itinerary without too much extra effort.
